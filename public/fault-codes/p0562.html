What P0562 Actually Means on BMW

The ECU constantly monitors the vehicle's electrical system voltage. P0562 is set when the voltage drops below a threshold that the ECU deems critical – typically below 11.5V during cranking or below 12.5V with the engine running. Crucially, the code describes a measurement, not a diagnosis. Low voltage could be caused by an ageing battery that no longer holds charge, a failing alternator that isn't charging, a loose or corroded connection, or even a parasitic drain. The distinction is the difference between a £100 battery and a £700 alternator, which is why the diagnostic order below matters more than the code itself. This code appears across all BMW petrol and diesel models – from N47 and N20 to B58 and N57 – and the diagnostic approach is essentially identical.

Symptoms You'll Notice Alongside It

  • Battery warning light illuminated on the dash
  • Slow cranking when starting the engine
  • Dim headlights or interior lights
  • Power steering may feel heavier than usual
  • Electronics behaving erratically – limp mode, warning lights flickering
  • Battery discharge warning on iDrive / infotainment system

Diagnostic Order — Cheapest First

1

Check battery voltage

Use a multimeter to measure battery voltage with the engine off (should be 12.5V+). Then start the car – voltage should rise to 13.5–14.5V.

2

Test battery condition

A proper battery load test will tell you if the battery can deliver enough cold cranking amps. Many factors, including age, are revealed.

3

Inspect terminals and ground

Remove, clean, and retighten battery terminals. Check the main chassis ground connection for corrosion.

4

Test alternator output

With the engine running, check voltage at the battery. If it's below 13.5V, the alternator or its wiring is suspect.

5

Check for parasitic drain

If the battery is good but drains overnight, a parasitic draw test identifies the offending circuit.

Can You Still Drive It?

Conditionally – only for very short distances. P0562 indicates the electrical system is struggling. If the alternator has failed, you may only have minutes of driving before the battery is depleted and the engine stalls. Modern BMWs with electric power steering, water pumps, and sensitive electronics can shut down unexpectedly. If you notice dim lights, warning messages, or the car feels sluggish, pull over safely and call for recovery. Do not attempt a long motorway journey – the risk of a sudden shutdown is real.

Related Fault Codes

P0562 often appears with other codes – the combination narrows the diagnosis significantly.

P0620 — Alternator Control Circuit

If both appear, suspect the alternator or its control wiring.

P0621 — Generator Lamp/L-Terminal Circuit

Indicates the warning light circuit is faulty – may be wiring or cluster issue.

P165B — BSD Communication Fault

BMW-specific code indicating communication with the alternator via BSD (Bit Serial Data) is lost.

Battery Warning Light

If the battery light is on while driving, the alternator is likely not charging.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I drive with P0562 on a BMW?

Only for very short distances to a garage. If the alternator has failed, the battery will discharge quickly and the car may stall. If you notice dim lights or warning messages, stop and call for recovery.

Does P0562 always mean the alternator has failed?

No. An ageing battery is far more common – especially on BMWs with stop-start systems that put extra strain on the battery. Always check the battery first.

Will clearing the code fix P0562?

Only temporarily. The code will return as soon as the voltage drops again – often within a few seconds of starting. Clearing it erases freeze‑frame data that helps with diagnosis.

How much does a BMW P0562 diagnosis cost?

Expect 1–2 hours of diagnostic time at an independent BMW specialist, typically £100–£200. Many specialists will waive or offset this if you proceed with the repair.

Is P0562 covered under BMW warranty or recall?

Batteries are considered consumable items and are rarely covered under warranty beyond the first 2–3 years. However, some alternator issues were subject to recalls on certain models. Check your VIN against BMW UK recalls or use our recall checker.
