What P164B Actually Means on BMW Diesels

P164B is a BMW-specific fault code that indicates the engine control unit (ECU) has detected a communication or control fault with the glow plug control module – sometimes called the glow plug relay or glow plug controller. The module is responsible for supplying power to the glow plugs for cold starts and post-heating during warm-up. The ECU communicates with the module via a data bus (often BSD – Bit Serial Data) and monitors its status. When the ECU loses communication, receives invalid data, or detects an internal fault in the module, it sets P164B. Crucially, the code describes a communication or control fault, not a hardware failure of the glow plugs themselves. The problem could be a poor connection, a broken wire, a blown fuse, or a failed module. This code appears across BMW diesel families – from the N47 and B47 four‑cylinder units to the N57 and B57 six‑cylinder engines – and the diagnostic approach is essentially identical.

Symptoms You'll Notice Alongside It

  • Glow plug warning light illuminated or flashing on the dash
  • Extended cranking time before the engine starts, especially in cold weather
  • Rough idle or misfire immediately after starting when cold
  • Reduced fuel economy
  • In some cases, white smoke from the exhaust on cold starts
  • No other drivability issues once the engine is warm

Diagnostic Order — Cheapest First

1

Check fuses and relays

Locate the glow plug control module fuse and relay in the engine bay fuse box. A blown fuse is a simple fix.

2

Inspect wiring and connector

Visually check the harness to the module for chafing, breaks, or corrosion. Wiggle the connector while monitoring live data.

3

Test module power and ground

Check for power supply and ground at the module. If they're present but no communication, the module is likely faulty.

4

Replace the glow plug control module

If fuses, wiring, and power are good, the module itself is the most likely failure point.

Can You Still Drive It?

Yes, but cold starts will be difficult. P164B does not cause immediate engine damage, but if the glow plugs aren't heating, the engine may struggle to start in cold weather. Once started and warmed up, the car will run normally. However, prolonged hard starting can stress the battery and starter motor. Get it diagnosed before winter sets in, but it's not an emergency in warmer months. If the engine won't start at all, the fault may be more severe – have it recovered.

Related Fault Codes

P164B often appears with other codes – the combination narrows the diagnosis significantly.

P0670 — Glow Plug Control Module Circuit

A more generic glow plug module code – if both appear, the module or its wiring is likely at fault.

P0671 — Glow Plug Cylinder 1 Circuit

If you have individual cylinder glow plug codes, the communication fault may be affecting the module's ability to control them.

P0672 — Glow Plug Cylinder 2 Circuit

Multiple cylinder codes suggest a module or common wiring issue.

Glow Plug Warning Light

If the light is flashing, it often indicates a serious glow system fault – check immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I drive with P164B on a BMW?

Yes, but cold starts will be difficult. Once the engine is warm, it runs normally. Avoid repeated hard starting to protect the battery and starter. Get it diagnosed soon.

Does P164B always mean the glow plug control module needs replacing?

No. A wiring issue or a blown fuse is far more common – both much cheaper than a new module. Always check those first.

Will clearing the code fix P164B?

Only temporarily. The code will return as soon as the ECU runs the glow plug system test, typically on the next cold start. Clearing it erases freeze‑frame data that helps with diagnosis.

How much does a BMW P164B diagnosis cost?

Expect 1–2 hours of diagnostic time at an independent BMW specialist, typically £100–£200. Many specialists will waive or offset this if you proceed with the repair.

Is P164B covered under BMW warranty or recall?

Glow plug systems are generally not covered by recalls, but some models had extended warranties on the glow plug module. Check your VIN against BMW UK recalls or use our recall checker.
