P0263 indicates cylinder 1 is not contributing as much power as the other cylinders – the DME has detected a fuel delivery, ignition, or compression imbalance. On most BMWs this is a fuel injector or coil pack issue, not a mechanical engine failure, and it should be diagnosed cheapest-first.

What P0263 Actually Means on a BMW

The engine control unit (DME) monitors the contribution of each cylinder to overall engine power by measuring crankshaft acceleration during each combustion event. P0263 is logged when cylinder 1's contribution is significantly lower than the other cylinders – the DME sees the crankshaft speed dip more than expected during cylinder 1's firing event. This is a balance code, not a component failure code. The cylinder could be underperforming because of a weak spark, a clogged or leaking injector, or a compression problem. The key to diagnosis is to rule out the cheap and common causes first – ignition coils, spark plugs, and wiring – before assuming expensive injector or engine damage. This code appears across the full range of BMW petrol and diesel engines, from the N47 to the B58, and the diagnostic approach is similar regardless of engine type.

Symptoms You'll Notice Alongside It

  • Engine management light illuminated, often amber
  • Limp mode or reduced power – the ECU may cut fuel to protect the engine
  • Rough idle or hesitation during acceleration
  • Engine vibration or shaking, especially at idle
  • Increased fuel consumption – the ECU may enrich the mixture to compensate

Diagnostic Order — Cheapest First

1

Swap ignition coil to another cylinder

Move the coil from cylinder 1 to a different cylinder. If the fault follows the coil, replace it – cheapest and most common fix.

2

Swap spark plug to another cylinder

If the coil swap doesn't help, swap the spark plug. A fouled or worn plug can cause imbalance.

3

Inspect wiring and connector for injector/coil

Check for corrosion or damaged wires at the cylinder 1 injector and coil connectors.

4

Run injector contribution test

Using a diagnostic tool, perform an injector cut-out test. If cylinder 1 shows less change than others, suspect the injector.

5

Perform compression test (cylinder 1)

If all electrical and fuel components check out, a compression test will confirm or rule out mechanical issues.

Can You Still Drive It?

Conditionally, yes – short journeys to get it diagnosed, but avoid heavy acceleration. P0263 on its own is not an immediate engine‑destroying fault, but continued driving with a misfiring or underperforming cylinder can damage the catalytic converter and increase fuel consumption. If the car has entered limp mode, motorway driving is unsafe due to lack of power. Get it looked at within a few days – ignoring a misfire can lead to injector or catalytic converter damage.

Related Fault Codes

P0263 rarely appears alone. Which codes accompany it materially changes the diagnosis.

P0300 — Random Misfire

Often accompanies P0263 if the misfire is severe enough.

P0301 — Cylinder 1 Misfire

Strong supporting evidence that cylinder 1 is the problem – usually a coil or plug.

P0261 — Injector Circuit Low (Cyl 1)

Points to an injector circuit issue rather than a mechanical problem.

P0171 — System Too Lean

Can occur if a leaking injector is causing a lean condition.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I drive with P0263 on a BMW?

For short journeys to a garage, generally yes – but not for normal use, and not on motorways if the vehicle has entered limp mode, because you won't be able to accelerate safely. Prolonged driving with a misfire can damage the catalytic converter.

Does P0263 always mean the injector has failed?

No. Across the six ranked causes, injector failure is only the fourth most common. Ignition coils and spark plugs are far more likely to fail and cost a fraction as much.

Will clearing the code fix it?

Only temporarily. The code will return as soon as the DME detects the imbalance again, usually within a few drive cycles. Clearing it also removes freeze‑frame data that helps pinpoint the cause.

How much should diagnosis cost?

Expect one to two hours of diagnostic time at an independent BMW specialist. Many will offset that cost against the repair if you proceed with them.

Is P0263 more common on petrol or diesel BMWs?

It appears on both, but petrol engines with individual ignition coils are more prone to coil failures that trigger this code. Diesel engines are more likely to have injector-related causes.
