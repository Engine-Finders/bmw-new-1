1 Series Engine Options – Which Do You Have?

The BMW 1 Series has used three main engine families: the N47 diesel, the B47 diesel, and the N57 six‑cylinder diesel (in 123d/125d models). Each has a different cost profile and write‑off risk.

The N47 is the most‑enquired engine on our platform, with 1,450 quote requests in 2025 [EM‑VERIFIED]. It was fitted to the E87 and early F20 1 Series. Its Achilles' heel is the rear‑mounted timing chain – a repair that costs £1,200–£2,500 but can escalate to a full engine replacement if ignored.

The B47 replaced the N47 in the F20 LCI and is standard in the F40. It's more reliable, with a front‑mounted chain that's easier and cheaper to replace. It generated 930 quote requests [EM‑VERIFIED] in 2025.

The N57 six‑cylinder diesel (found in 123d and 125d models) has its own cost profile – higher repair bills but also higher values, which can make replacement viable on the right car.

The key question: does your car's value justify the repair? That's where the 70% rule comes in.

The 70% Rule Applied to 1 Series Models

We use the BMW‑specific 70% repair‑to‑value threshold. If a repair costs more than 70% of your car's current market value, the honest verdict is to scrap or sell for parts.

Here's how it works for the most common 1 Series generations, using typical 2025 UK values [THIRD-PARTY].

Example 1: 2008 E87 118d (N47) – Value £3,500

Repair: Timing chain replacement – ~£2,000

Ratio: £2,000 ÷ £3,500 = 57% → ✅ Safe (borderline but under 70%)

But if the same car needs a full engine replacement at £5,500:

Ratio: £5,500 ÷ £3,500 = 157% → 🔴 Scrap

Example 2: 2014 F20 118d (N47) – Value £7,000

Full engine replacement: £5,500

Ratio: £5,500 ÷ £7,000 = 79% → ⚠️ Watch (just over 70%)

At £8,000 value, the ratio drops to 69% → ✅ Safe. At £6,000, it's 92% → 🔴 Scrap.

The verdict shifts with just a few hundred pounds in valuation – always check your own figure.

Example 3: 2019 F40 118d (B47) – Value £16,000

Full engine replacement: £4,500

Ratio: £4,500 ÷ £16,000 = 28% → ✅ Safe

Even at £10,000 value, the ratio is 45% → ✅ Safe.

Frequently Asked Questions – 1 Series Write‑Off

Is it worth replacing an engine in a 1 Series?

It depends on the generation and engine. For an F40 with a B47 engine, yes – the car's value easily justifies it. For an E87 or early F20 with an N47, a full engine replacement is often a write‑off, but a timing chain repair is usually worth it.

What is the most common 1 Series engine failure?

The N47 timing chain failure is the most common and most expensive if ignored. It's the main reason 1 Series enquiries are so high – 1,450 quote requests in 2025 [EM‑VERIFIED].

How much does a used 1 Series engine cost?

A used N47 supply‑only costs £1,800–£3,200, plus fitting (£650–£910). A reconditioned N47 is £3,200–£5,500, plus fitting. Total fitted cost often exceeds £4,500–£6,500 [EM‑QUOTE].

Is the B47 engine in the F40 more reliable than the N47?

Yes. The B47 has a front‑mounted timing chain that's far more durable and easier to replace. It also has fewer known failure points. That's why F40 1 Series models are almost always worth repairing.
