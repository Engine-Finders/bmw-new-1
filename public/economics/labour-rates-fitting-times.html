What Affects Fitting Time and Labour Cost?

Not all engine swaps are equal. Several factors can increase the hours and the final bill.

🚗

Vehicle Platform

X‑models (SUV) often have more room but can require extra disassembly (subframe, suspension). 7‑Series and 5‑Series may have more electronics to disconnect.

🔧

Engine Complexity

V8s (N63/S63) take longer due to tight engine bays, twin turbos, and hot‑vee cooling lines. 4‑cylinders are simpler and quicker.

🛠️

Additional Work

If the replacement includes new ancillaries (turbo, injectors, coolant pipes), the time increases. A full “long block” swap is faster than swapping over components.

📍

Location

Workshops in London/SE charge higher rates. Mobile mechanics may have lower rates but may lack the equipment for a full engine swap.

How Labour Fits into the 70% Rule

Labour is a major part of the total repair cost. Our 70% repair‑to‑value threshold includes all supply‑and‑fit costs, so labour hours directly affect the decision.

For example, an N47 engine replacement with a reconditioned engine at £3,200–£5,500 supply plus £650–£910 fitting gives a total of £3,850–£6,410. On a car worth £6,000, that puts the ratio at 64–107% – often exceeding the 70% line. The labour portion alone can push a borderline case into the scrap zone.

On the other hand, a B58 replacement on a newer G‑series car with high value usually stays well under 70%, even with dealer labour rates.

Getting a Reliable Quote – What to Ask

When you approach a specialist, be clear about what is included. Here are the questions we recommend asking every workshop.

  • Is the labour rate fixed or estimated? – Get a fixed-price labour quote if possible.
  • What is included? – Engine oil, coolant, filters, new bolts, gaskets? Some specialists include these, others add them as extras.
  • Is the price for the engine supply‑only or supply‑and‑fit? – Always compare supply‑and‑fit totals.
  • Do you provide a warranty on labour? – Typically 12 months on parts and labour from reputable specialists.
  • What is the turnaround time? – Engine swaps usually take 1–3 days depending on complexity.

Frequently Asked Questions – Labour & Fitting

How much do BMW specialists charge per hour?

Independent BMW specialists in the UK typically charge £65–£90 per hour outside London, and £90–£130 in London/South East. Main dealers charge £120–£160 per hour. Always confirm before booking.

How long does it take to fit a BMW engine?

Fitting time ranges from 10–14 hours for a 4‑cylinder N47/B47, 12–16 hours for a 6‑cylinder N57/B57, and 16–22 hours for a V8 N63. More complex M‑engines can take up to 24 hours.

Can a mobile mechanic replace a BMW engine?

While some mobile mechanics can do the job, engine swaps require a ramp, engine hoist, and specialist diagnostic tools. Most reputable specialists prefer to do the work in a workshop setting. We recommend using a vetted workshop from our network.

Do labour rates affect the 70% write‑off decision?

Yes. Labour can add 30‑50% to the total cost. Our 70% threshold is based on the full supply‑and‑fit price, so higher labour rates can push a repair over the threshold. Always get a total quote before deciding.
