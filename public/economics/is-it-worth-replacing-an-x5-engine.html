X5 Generations and Engine Options

The BMW X5 has used a wide range of engines across its generations. The most common in our data are the N57 six‑cylinder diesel (E70, F15), N63 V8 petrol (E70, F15), B57 six‑cylinder diesel (G05), and the B58 inline‑six petrol (G05). Each has a different cost profile and write‑off risk.

The N57 is a very popular diesel, with 830 quote requests across all BMW models in 2025 [EM‑VERIFIED]. It was fitted to the E70 and F15 X5. Its Achilles' heel is the rear‑mounted timing chain – a repair that costs £1,500–£2,500 but can escalate to a full engine replacement if ignored.

The N63 V8 is known for its hot‑vee design and associated heat‑related failures. It generated 440 quote requests [EM‑VERIFIED] in 2025. Repair costs are high, and replacement often pushes the 70% threshold on older X5s.

The B57 and B58 are the modern successors, found in the G05 X5. They are significantly more reliable, with fewer catastrophic failure modes. The B57 has 460 quote requests [EM‑VERIFIED] and the B58 has 540 [EM‑VERIFIED] across all models.

The key question: does your car's value justify the repair? That's where the 70% rule comes in.

The 70% Rule Applied to X5 Models

We use the BMW‑specific 70% repair‑to‑value threshold. If a repair costs more than 70% of your car's current market value, the honest verdict is to scrap or sell for parts.

Here's how it works for the most common X5 generations, using typical 2025 UK values [THIRD-PARTY].

Example 1: 2008 E70 3.0d (N57) – Value £6,000

Repair: Timing chain replacement – ~£2,000

Ratio: £2,000 ÷ £6,000 = 33% → ✅ Safe (well under 70%)

But if the same car needs a full engine replacement at £7,500:

Ratio: £7,500 ÷ £6,000 = 125% → 🔴 Scrap

Example 2: 2013 F15 50i (N63) – Value £12,000

Valve stem seal repair: £4,500

Ratio: £4,500 ÷ £12,000 = 37% → ✅ Safe

But full engine replacement at £10,000:

Ratio: £10,000 ÷ £12,000 = 83% → 🔴 Scrap

Example 3: 2019 G05 30d (B57) – Value £30,000

Full engine replacement: £7,500

Ratio: £7,500 ÷ £30,000 = 25% → ✅ Safe

Even at £15,000 value, the ratio is 50% → ✅ Safe.

Frequently Asked Questions – X5 Write‑Off

Is it worth replacing an engine in an X5?

It depends on the generation and engine. For a G05 with a B57 or B58 engine, yes – the car's value easily justifies it. For an E70 or early F15 with an N57 or N63, a full engine replacement is often a write‑off, but a timing chain repair is usually worth it.

What is the most common X5 engine failure?

The N57 timing chain failure is the most common and most expensive if ignored. It's the main reason X5 enquiries are so high – 830 quote requests for the N57 in 2025 [EM‑VERIFIED].

How much does a used X5 engine cost?

A used N57 supply‑only costs £2,500–£4,500, plus fitting (£780–£1,040). A reconditioned N63 is £6,000–£10,000, plus fitting. Total fitted cost often exceeds £7,000–£12,000 [EM‑QUOTE].

Is the B57 engine in the G05 more reliable than the N57?

Yes. The B57 is a modern design with a front‑mounted timing chain and fewer known failure points. That's why G05 X5 models are almost always worth repairing.
