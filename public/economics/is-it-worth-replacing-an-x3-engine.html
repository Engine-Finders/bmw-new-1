X3 Generations and Engine Options

The BMW X3 has used a range of engines across its generations. The most common are the N47 diesel (E83, early F25), N57 six‑cylinder diesel (F25), B47 diesel (F25 LCI, G01), and the B57 six‑cylinder diesel (G01). Each has a different cost profile and write‑off risk.

The N47 is the most‑enquired engine on our platform, with 1,450 quote requests in 2025 [EM‑VERIFIED]. It was fitted to the E83 and early F25 X3. Its Achilles' heel is the rear‑mounted timing chain – a repair that costs £1,200–£2,500 but can escalate to a full engine replacement if ignored.

The N57 six‑cylinder diesel is found in the 30d and 35d models. It has its own cost profile – higher repair bills but also higher values. It generated 830 quote requests [EM‑VERIFIED] in 2025.

The B47 replaced the N47 in the F25 LCI and is standard in the G01. It's more reliable, with a front‑mounted chain that's easier and cheaper to replace. It generated 930 quote requests [EM‑VERIFIED] in 2025.

The B57 is the latest six‑cylinder diesel, found in the G01 X3. It is generally reliable, with fewer catastrophic failure modes than the N‑series engines.

The key question: does your car's value justify the repair? That's where the 70% rule comes in.

The 70% Rule Applied to X3 Models

We use the BMW‑specific 70% repair‑to‑value threshold. If a repair costs more than 70% of your car's current market value, the honest verdict is to scrap or sell for parts.

Here's how it works for the most common X3 generations, using typical 2025 UK values [THIRD-PARTY].

Example 1: 2006 E83 2.0d (N47) – Value £3,500

Repair: Timing chain replacement – ~£2,000

Ratio: £2,000 ÷ £3,500 = 57% → ✅ Safe (borderline but under 70%)

But if the same car needs a full engine replacement at £5,500:

Ratio: £5,500 ÷ £3,500 = 157% → 🔴 Scrap

Example 2: 2012 F25 20d (N47) – Value £8,000

Full engine replacement: £5,500

Ratio: £5,500 ÷ £8,000 = 69% → ✅ Safe (just under 70%)

At £7,000 value, the ratio jumps to 79% → ⚠️ Watch. At £6,000, it's 92% → 🔴 Scrap.

The verdict shifts with just a few hundred pounds in valuation – always check your own figure.

Example 3: 2018 G01 20d (B47) – Value £20,000

Full engine replacement: £4,500

Ratio: £4,500 ÷ £20,000 = 22% → ✅ Safe

Even at £10,000 value, the ratio is 45% → ✅ Safe.

Frequently Asked Questions – X3 Write‑Off

Is it worth replacing an engine in an X3?

It depends on the generation and engine. For a G01 with a B47 or B57 engine, yes – the car's value easily justifies it. For an E83 or early F25 with an N47, a full engine replacement is often a write‑off, but a timing chain repair is usually worth it.

What is the most common X3 engine failure?

The N47 timing chain failure is the most common and most expensive if ignored. It's the main reason X3 enquiries are so high – 1,450 quote requests for the N47 in 2025 [EM‑VERIFIED].

How much does a used X3 engine cost?

A used N47 supply‑only costs £1,800–£3,200, plus fitting (£650–£910). A reconditioned N57 is £4,500–£7,000, plus fitting. Total fitted cost often exceeds £5,000–£8,000 [EM‑QUOTE].

Is the B47 engine in the G01 more reliable than the N47?

Yes. The B47 is a modern design with a front‑mounted timing chain and fewer known failure points. That's why G01 X3 models are almost always worth repairing.
