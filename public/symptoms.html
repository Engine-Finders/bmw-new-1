Symptom Index

Click any symptom below to go to its dedicated page. Each page covers that symptom across the entire BMW range, with engine-specific cuts where the data supports it.

Cold-Start Rattle

Rattle on startup that fades as the engine warms. Often points to timing chain tensioner wear on N47 and N57 engines.

Black Smoke From Exhaust

Thick black smoke under acceleration. Points to over-fuelling, faulty injectors, or a failing MAF sensor.

Blue Smoke From Exhaust

Blue smoke indicates burning oil. Common causes include worn turbo seals, valve stem seals, or piston rings.

White Smoke From Exhaust

White smoke that smells sweet points to coolant ingress. Head gasket failure is the most common cause.

Engine Knocking Noise

A deep knocking or thumping sound. Often a sign of crankshaft bearing wear or fuel detonation.

Engine Overheating

Temperature gauge rising above normal. Causes include coolant leaks, failed water pump, or a stuck thermostat.

Coolant Loss — No Visible Leak

Coolant level dropping without a visible puddle. Often points to EGR cooler failure or a head gasket leak.

Oil in Coolant

Mayonnaise-like sludge in the coolant expansion tank. Typically caused by a failed oil cooler or head gasket.

Oil Dilution / Fuel in Oil

Oil level rising and smelling of diesel. Common on diesel engines with DPF regeneration issues.

Excessive Oil Consumption

Needing to top up oil between services. Can be caused by worn piston rings, valve seals, or turbo seals.

Limp Mode / Reduced Power

Car enters limp mode with the engine management light on. Can be caused by a range of sensor or actuator faults.

Misfire or Rough Idle

Engine runs unevenly, especially at idle. Often caused by a faulty ignition coil, injector, or vacuum leak.

Rattle Under Acceleration

A metallic rattle when accelerating. Can be caused by a timing chain issue, turbocharger wear, or exhaust heat shield.

Turbo Whistle

A high-pitched whistle from the turbocharger. Often a sign of worn bearings or a boost leak.

Cranks But Won't Start

Engine cranks but won't fire. Causes include fuel supply issues, sensor failure, or immobiliser problems.

Engine-specific symptom pages — such as N47 cold-start rattle or B47 black smoke — are also available for engines where the symptom behaves differently. See the individual pages for details.
