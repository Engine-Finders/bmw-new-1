The Seven Criteria

Every one of these is checked before a specialist receives a single enquiry from us.

#CriterionWhat We Actually Check
1Genuine JLR specialismDemonstrable regular work on Land Rover, Range Rover and Jaguar powertrains — not a general garage that will accept the job
2Minimum trading historyEstablished trading record, verifiable through Companies House and trading address
3Written warranty on supply & fitA stated, documented warranty period on both the unit and the labour — see our warranty standards
4Engine provenanceAbility to state where a reconditioned or used unit came from and to what standard it was rebuilt
5Response timeConsistent response to enquiries within a working day — slow responders are removed from routing
6Complaint handlingA documented process for when a job goes wrong, and evidence of having used it
7Insurance & premisesCurrent liability cover and a permanent, inspectable workshop address

What Disqualifies a Specialist

Any one of these removes a supplier from the network, regardless of how well they score elsewhere.

🚫 No written warranty

A verbal assurance is not a warranty. If it isn't documented, the specialist doesn't join.

🚫 Quoting without diagnosis

Any supplier willing to quote a full engine replacement without seeing the vehicle or the diagnostic evidence.

🚫 Unverifiable engine provenance

If they can't say where a unit came from or to what standard it was rebuilt, we don't route to them.

🚫 Pattern of unresolved complaints

Isolated problems happen in any trade. A pattern of them, or a refusal to engage with them, is disqualifying.

How We're Paid, Stated Plainly

This site is free to use because specialists pay us for the enquiries we send them. You pay us nothing, and we take no commission on the work itself.

We'd rather explain that openly than let you assume the site runs on goodwill. And it's precisely why our editorial independence matters: if we only ever told people to replace their engine, we'd generate more enquiries in the short term and destroy the reason anyone trusts us in the long term. That's why our repair vs replace framework is published in full — including the 60% threshold above which we tell people not to replace — and why pages like is it worth replacing a Freelander engine and when should you scrap a Land Rover exist at all. Our full position is set out in our editorial standards.

Ongoing Review, Not One-Off Vetting

Vetting at the point of joining is the easy part. Specialists are reviewed continuously against response time, completion rate and post-job outcomes collected through follow-up surveys — the same data source behind our published case studies and our verified owner outcomes. Where a specialist's outcomes deteriorate, routing is reduced or stopped. Our data handling and tagging is documented on the data verification page, and any corrections we've had to make are logged publicly in our corrections log.

What Happens When a Specialist Is Removed

Removal isn't a single strike process. A specialist falling below the response-time or completion-rate bar first receives reduced routing rather than immediate removal, giving them a chance to correct course — most do. Full removal happens where a disqualifying issue is confirmed, such as an unresolved complaint pattern or a warranty dispute the specialist refused to engage with. We don't publish the names of removed specialists, both for straightforward legal reasons and because our purpose is steering future enquiries well, not running a public complaints board — but the removal itself is real and immediate once confirmed, and no specialist has ever been reinstated after a disqualifying removal.

What We Are Not

We are not a garage, we are not a dealership, and we are not affiliated with Jaguar Land Rover Limited. We don't hold engine stock, we don't carry out repairs, and we never present ourselves as the party doing the work. What we do is research — our annual failure index and published methodology are the clearest statement of that — and then connect owners with specialists who do. If you'd like to know more about the people behind the research, our expert panel page sets out who reviews what, and how it works walks through what happens after you make an enquiry.
