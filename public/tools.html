Frequently Asked Questions About BMW Tools

Are all these BMW tools really free?

Yes — every tool on this site is completely free and does not require an email address to see your result. We ask for an email only if you want to save a result or compare quotes.

What data powers the BMW tools?

Our tools are powered by the same 2025 UK enquiry data from EnginesMarket.co.uk that runs every page on this site — 9,000+ real UK enquiries, supplier quotes, and specialist interviews. See our Data Methodology for full details.

What does the 70% scrap threshold mean for BMW?

The 70% threshold means if the total cost of a repair (including labour) exceeds 70% of your vehicle's current market value, we recommend evaluating scrap or part-exchange before committing to a repair. This is the BMW-specific threshold derived from real UK enquiry data. See the Decision Framework for the full worked example.

Which BMW tools should I use first?

If you have your registration, start with Registration Lookup to identify your engine instantly. If you have your VIN, use VIN Decoder for the full build spec. For decision-making, use Repair vs Replace Calculator — it's the most visited tool on the site.

Do the tools work for M models?

Yes — all tools support M models including M3, M4, M5, M6, M8, X3M, X4M, X5M, X6M and XM. The Repair vs Replace Calculator uses M-specific value bands and replacement costs from the proprietary 2025 data.

Can I use these tools on mobile?

Yes — all tools are mobile-first and fully responsive. The calculators and lookup tools work on any screen size, and the results are optimised for reading on a phone.
