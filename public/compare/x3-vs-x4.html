The Verdict

For most buyers, the X3 is the stronger choice. It generates more enquiries, offers significantly more rear headroom and cargo space, and is generally better value on the used market. The X4 is a brilliant car if you're willing to sacrifice practicality for a coupe silhouette — but for families or anyone who regularly carries passengers or luggage, the X3 is the obvious winner. Both share the same engine line-up and reliability record, so the decision comes down to lifestyle and preference. The 70% BMW scrap threshold means replacement economics are similar, but the X3's higher sales volume makes parts and specialist knowledge more widely available [EM-INTERVIEW].

X3 Deep Dive

The BMW X3 is the brand's best‑selling SUV, available in three generations (E83, F25, G01). The F25 (2011–2017) is the most‑enquired generation in our data, with 316 enquiries in 2025 [EM-VERIFIED], while the G01 (2018–present) adds another 182 enquiries [EM-VERIFIED]. Engines range from the N47 and B47 four‑cylinder diesels to the N57 and B57 six‑cylinder diesels, and petrol options including the N20 and B48. The X3 is renowned for its balance of comfort, handling, and practicality — rear seats fold 40:20:40, and the boot offers 550 litres, significantly more than the X4's 500 litres (and the sloping roof cuts rear headroom notably). The N47 Knowledge Centre covers the diesel powertrains, while the N47 failure rate data shows the X3 is a common host. For most families, the X3 is the sensible, capable choice.

X4 Deep Dive

The X4 is the coupe‑SUV version of the X3, sharing the same platform and engines but with a dramatically sloping roofline. The first‑generation F26 (2014–2018) generated 143 enquiries in 2025 [EM-VERIFIED], while the second‑generation G02 (2018–present) is less common in our data but similarly equipped. The X4 trades rear headroom and cargo capacity for a more striking design and slightly lower centre of gravity, which improves handling feel. Engines are identical to the X3 — the B47 and B57 are the most common replacements. The X4 is a great choice if you prioritise style and driving dynamics over outright practicality, but you pay a premium for that design. Our repair vs replace framework applies equally to both models, though the X4's lower production numbers can make second‑hand parts slightly harder to find.

Five‑Year Ownership Cost

Beyond the purchase price, the running costs of the X3 and X4 are nearly identical — they share the same engines, transmissions, and most mechanical components. Fuel economy, insurance groups, and servicing costs are within a few percent of each other. The main difference is depreciation: the X4 tends to hold its value slightly better due to its sportier appeal, but it also costs more to buy initially. The X3's higher sales volume means more used examples are available, which can make it cheaper to find a well‑specified car. When it comes to engine replacement, the cost for a reconditioned N47 or B47 is £3,200–£5,500 supply‑only [EM-QUOTE], and labour is 10–14 hours for either model. Both are comfortably ✅ Safe against typical vehicle values, though the 70% BMW threshold means older, lower‑value examples may be borderline — see our scrap guide for details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the X3 better than the X4?

For most buyers, yes — the X3 is more practical, better value, and shares the same engines and reliability. The X4 is a style choice.

Which is more reliable, X3 or X4?

They are equally reliable — they share the same engines (N47, B47, N57, B57, etc.) and platforms. Reliability depends on engine choice and maintenance history rather than the model.

Is the X4 cheaper to insure than the X3?

Insurance groups are similar, though the X4 may be slightly higher due to its sportier image. Always check specific quotes.

Which has more boot space, X3 or X4?

The X3 has around 550 litres, while the X4 has about 500 litres — the sloping roofline reduces cargo capacity and rear headroom.

Which is cheaper to run, X3 or X4?

Running costs are nearly identical — same engines, same fuel economy, same servicing. Depreciation may favour the X4 slightly but purchase prices are higher.

Is the X4 worth the extra money?

Only if you value the coupe design and are willing to sacrifice practicality. For most buyers, the X3 offers better value.
