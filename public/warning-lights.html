Frequently Asked Questions

What does the engine management light mean on a BMW?

It indicates the ECU has detected a fault — usually emissions, fuel, ignition or sensor related. It can be a simple O2 sensor or a more serious timing chain issue. Always read the fault code.

Is it safe to drive with the DPF light on?

If it's just the DPF light (amber) and no other warnings, you can drive — but you need to complete a regeneration cycle by driving at motorway speed for 30 minutes. If the light is flashing red or accompanied by limp mode, stop and get a specialist to inspect it.

What should I do if the oil pressure light comes on?

Stop the car as soon as it's safe and turn off the engine. Low oil pressure can cause catastrophic engine damage in minutes. Check the oil level; if it's low, top up and restart cautiously. If the light persists, call for recovery.

How do I know if a warning light is serious?

Red lights mean stop immediately; amber means get it checked soon. The severity guide on each page gives clear yes/no/conditional advice based on BMW engine family and chassis.
