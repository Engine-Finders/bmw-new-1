Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between failure rate and enquiry volume?

Enquiry volume is the raw number of quote requests we received for a given engine. It reflects both the popularity of that engine (how many are on the road) and its failure frequency. We never claim enquiry volume equals failure rate – our engine‑specific pages provide context to help you interpret the numbers.

How often is your data updated?

Our core dataset is updated annually, each February, covering the previous full calendar year. We also publish interim trend updates when significant patterns emerge, but the annual report is our definitive release.

Can I trust these numbers?

Yes – every number on our site carries a data tag (e.g., [EM-VERIFIED]) that tells you exactly where it came from. Our methodology is published in full, and we are transparent about limitations. We do not invent numbers.

How do you define "failure" in your data?

A failure is any enquiry that resulted in a quote request for an engine replacement, rebuild, or major component (e.g., timing chain, turbo). We do not include routine servicing or minor repairs. Full definition is in our methodology page.

Where can I find data for older BMW engines?

We have data for older engines like M47, M57, N62, and N54 – these are included in the engine‑specific index above. If you don't see your engine, it's likely because we didn't receive enough enquiries for a meaningful dataset; we only publish data when we have a minimum sample size.
