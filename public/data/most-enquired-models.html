Methodology

How we built this model enquiry data.

Source: Internal CRM quote-request data from EnginesMarket.co.uk and EngineFinders.co.uk, combined. Full calendar year 2025. 24,650 unique quote requests across all brands; 5,060 relate to BMW engines. Model-level data is derived from customer vehicle details collected at the point of enquiry.

What counts as a "model enquiry": a unique customer quote request specifying a particular BMW model (e.g., 3 Series F30). Repeat requests from the same customer for the same model are not double-counted.

What this data cannot tell you: model enquiry volume reflects both the popularity of a model and its failure frequency. A higher volume can mean a larger vehicle parc, older vehicles reaching replacement age, or a genuine reliability issue. We do not claim failure rates against the total vehicle population from model data alone.

Model Analysis

What the model ranking tells us about BMW ownership and reliability.

The 3 Series F30's position at the top is unsurprising — it's one of BMW's most popular models, with a huge vehicle parc across the UK. Its high enquiry volume reflects both its popularity and the N47 timing chain issues that affect many pre-2016 examples [PATTERN DATA]. The 1 Series F20 and X3 F25 follow closely, sharing similar engines and vulnerability to the same N47 timing chain weakness.

The presence of older models like the 3 Series E90 and 1 Series E87 at positions 6 and 7 is notable. These models have lower vehicle values (£2,500–£7,500 and £1,500–£5,000 respectively), which means replacement costs can exceed vehicle value — flagged as Scrap Threshold in our R-to-V analysis [THIRD-PARTY]. For owners of these models, the decision to replace or scrap is often a close call.

The newer G-series models (G20, G30, G05, G01, F40) show significantly lower enquiry volumes relative to their vehicle parc size, reflecting the improved reliability of the B-series engines. The B47 and B57 are notably more reliable than their N-series predecessors [PATTERN DATA].

What This Means For Owners

The most-enquired models reflect both popularity and reliability. The 3 Series F30's top position is driven by its massive vehicle parc as much as any reliability issue. For owners of F30, F20 and F25 models, the key takeaway is to be aware of N47 timing chain issues and to budget accordingly.

For owners of E90 and E87 models, the Scrap Threshold flag is a genuine financial warning. With vehicle values often below £4,000, a reconditioned engine replacement at £3,200–£5,500 can be a borderline decision. It's worth using our Repair vs Replace Calculator to see if replacement makes financial sense for your specific vehicle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which BMW model generates the most engine enquiries?

The BMW 3 Series F30 leads with 380 enquiries in our 2025 data, followed by the 1 Series F20 at 344 and the X3 F25 at 316. This reflects the popularity of these models as much as any reliability issues.

Why are older BMW models like the E90 and E87 on the list?

These models have large vehicle parcs and are now reaching the age where engine replacements become common. Their lower vehicle values make replacement a borderline financial decision, as flagged by our Scrap Threshold analysis.

Do newer G-series models have fewer enquiries?

Yes. The G20, G30, G05, G01 and F40 models show significantly lower enquiry volumes relative to their vehicle parc size, reflecting the improved reliability of the B-series engines. The B47 and B57 are notably more reliable than their N-series predecessors.

What does the R-to-V flag mean?

The R-to-V (Repair-to-Value) flag shows whether replacing an engine is financially sensible. ✅ Safe means the replacement cost is well within the vehicle's value. ⚠️ Watch means the cost may be 40-70% of the vehicle's value. 🔴 Scrap means the cost may exceed the vehicle's value — evaluate scrap before committing.

Is this data representative of all BMWs on UK roads?

No, and we want to be upfront about that. Our dataset reflects people who contacted us about a replacement engine, which naturally over-represents models that fail and under-represents ones that don't. Full methodology and limitations are set out in this report.
