What a Recall Does NOT Cover – and What You Pay For

A recall is a manufacturer-mandated fix for a safety or emissions defect. It is always free to the owner, regardless of mileage or age, provided the car is within the recall VIN range.

However, not every problem is a recall. Common issues that are not covered include:

  • Wear and tear – brakes, clutch, suspension, tyres.
  • Maintenance neglect – oil changes, filters, belts that have not been replaced per schedule.
  • Known mechanical failures without a recall – e.g., N63 valve stem seals are a known issue but not always covered by recall; full repair is usually owner-paid.
  • Damage from external causes – accident, impact, flood damage.

If you're unsure whether your symptom is a recall or a common fault, browse our symptom guides or use the Diagnostic Wizard.

Tip: Always check for outstanding recalls before paying for a repair – you could be eligible for a free fix.

Frequently Asked Questions about BMW 7 Series Recalls

How do I check if my BMW 7 Series has an outstanding recall?

Use our VIN Decoder – enter your 17‑digit VIN and we'll show you any open campaigns. You can also check the official DVSA recall checker or BMW UK website.

What is the Takata airbag recall on the 7 Series?

Certain 7 Series models (E65/E66) have a recall for the driver's airbag inflator, which may rupture in a crash. Dealers replace the airbag module free of charge.

Do I have to pay for a recall repair?

No. If your car is within the affected VIN range, the repair is free at any BMW dealer or authorised specialist. This includes parts and labour.

How long does a recall repair take?

It varies – some are quick (airbag replacement takes about an hour), while complex campaigns like EGR cooler replacement may take a full day. Your dealer will give a timeframe when you book.

Can a recall fix a failed engine?

Only if the failure is directly caused by the recall defect. For example, if a timing chain tensioner recall was not performed and the chain later fails, BMW may cover the damage under goodwill. But normal wear is not covered.

What should I do if I missed a recall?

Recalls are generally valid indefinitely – you can still get the repair done free of charge, even if you are not the original owner. Contact a BMW dealer with your VIN.
