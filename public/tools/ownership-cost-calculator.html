What Your BMW Ownership Cost Results Mean

The calculator shows a 5-year total cost broken down into six categories. Fuel is based on UK average fuel prices and your annual mileage. Maintenance covers repairs, tyres and unexpected costs. Servicing is scheduled maintenance at BMW specialists. Insurance uses UK average premiums for each model. VED is vehicle excise duty based on emissions. Depreciation estimates the value loss over five years.

Use these figures alongside our cheapest BMWs to own guide to find a model that fits your budget. For deeper cost analysis, see our replacement cost by model data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the BMW ownership cost calculator free?

Yes — completely free with no email required to see your results. We ask for an email only if you want to save results or compare quotes.

How accurate are the cost estimates?

Very accurate for UK-market BMW models — based on 2025 data. Actual costs vary by region, driving style and individual vehicle condition. Always get a specialist quote for specific costs.

What does "5-year ownership cost" include?

It includes fuel, maintenance, servicing, insurance, VED and depreciation over five years. It does not include finance costs, parking or tolls.

Can I compare costs between different BMW models?

Yes — run the calculator for different models and compare the 5-year totals side by side. For more detailed comparisons, see our comparison pages.

What's the difference between diesel and petrol running costs?

Diesel typically offers better fuel economy but higher maintenance and potential DPF issues. Petrol is generally cheaper to maintain but less fuel-efficient. See our diesel vs petrol comparison for full details.
