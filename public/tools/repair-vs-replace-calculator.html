What Your Repair vs Replace Decision Means

The calculator applies the 70% BMW scrap threshold — if the total repair cost (including labour) is less than 70% of your vehicle's current market value, repair is recommended. If it's between 70% and 100%, watch — consider a second opinion and compare replacement costs. If it exceeds 100%, scrap or part-exchange is usually the better financial decision.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the BMW repair vs replace calculator free?

Yes — completely free with no email required to see your results. We ask for an email only if you want to save results or compare quotes.

What is the 70% BMW scrap threshold?

The 70% threshold means if the total cost of a repair (including labour) exceeds 70% of your vehicle's current market value, we recommend evaluating scrap or part-exchange before committing to a repair. This is the BMW-specific threshold derived from real UK enquiry data. See the Decision Framework for the full worked example.

What should I use for the vehicle value?

Use a realistic current market value for your BMW — check AutoTrader, Parkers or our engine value checker. For high-mileage or modified cars, use a conservative estimate.

What does the calculator recommend if I'm between 70% and 100%?

It will recommend "Watch" — get a second opinion, compare replacement costs, and consider your personal attachment to the car. Some BMWs are worth more to their owners than the market suggests.

Does this work for M models?

Yes — select M3, M5 or other M models from the dropdown. M models use different value bands and replacement costs, reflecting their higher value and specialist repair costs.
