What Your BMW Oil Finder Results Mean

The tool shows the BMW Longlife oil specification that meets the requirements for your engine. LL-01 is the older standard for petrol engines. LL-04 is for diesel engines with DPF. LL-12 and LL-17 FE+ are the latest standards for modern engines, offering lower viscosity and better fuel economy.

Using the wrong oil specification can lead to timing chain stretch, sludge buildup, DPF damage and eventual engine failure. Always use the specified oil and change it at the recommended interval — see our service schedule pages for model-specific guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the BMW oil finder free?

Yes — completely free with no email required to see your results. We ask for an email only if you want to save results or compare quotes.

What BMW Longlife oil spec do I need?

It depends on your engine and model year — the tool shows the exact spec for your engine code. As a general guide, LL-01 is for older petrols, LL-04 for diesels with DPF, LL-12 for some modern diesels, and LL-17 FE+ for the latest petrol and diesel engines.

Can I use a different oil spec if I can't find the right one?

No — using the wrong oil specification can cause timing chain stretch, sludge buildup and DPF damage. Always use the BMW-approved spec shown by the tool. If you can't find the right oil, contact one of our 20+ vetted specialists for advice.

What's the oil change interval for my BMW?

The tool shows the recommended interval for your engine — typically 15,000–18,000 miles or 2 years for most modern BMWs. However, many specialists recommend shorter intervals (8,000–10,000 miles) for high-mileage or performance engines.
