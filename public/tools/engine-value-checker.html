What Your Engine Value Results Mean

The checker shows the supply-only price range for your selected engine and condition. Used engines are removed from donor vehicles — cheapest but variable quality. Reconditioned engines are professionally rebuilt to good working order — most common choice [EM-VERIFIED]. Rebuilt engines are fully stripped and rebuilt with new parts — highest quality and price.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the BMW engine value checker free?

Yes — completely free with no email required to see your results. We ask for an email only if you want to save results or compare quotes.

How accurate are the value ranges?

Very accurate for UK-market BMW engines — based on 2025 supplier quotes and enquiry data. Prices vary by region, mileage and specific condition; always get a specialist quote before committing.

What's the difference between reconditioned and rebuilt?

Reconditioned means the engine is professionally cleaned, inspected and any worn parts replaced to make it serviceable. Rebuilt means the engine is fully stripped, all critical components replaced and built to factory tolerances — higher quality and price. See our guide to engine conditions.

Can I check the value of an engine not listed?

If your engine isn't in the dropdown, it may be less common or older. Use our engine code guide to identify it, then check similar engines or contact one of our 20+ vetted specialists for a custom quote.

How does this tool help me decide whether to repair or scrap?

By showing the replacement cost, you can compare it to your vehicle's current value. Use our repair vs replace calculator to apply the 70% BMW scrap threshold and see a clear recommendation.
