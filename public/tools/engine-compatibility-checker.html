What Your Compatibility Results Mean

The checker lists every engine code that was factory-fitted to your selected model, chassis and year. Engine codes are shown with displacement, fuel type and power output. Fitment note indicates whether the engine is a direct bolt-in or may require additional parts (ECU, wiring, mounts).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the BMW engine compatibility checker free?

Yes — completely free with no email required to see your results. We ask for an email only if you want to save results or compare quotes.

How accurate is the compatibility data?

Very high for UK-market BMW models. We use factory fitment data and 2025 enquiry patterns. For rare or imported models, results may be limited — check our Fitment Matrix for more detail.

Can I use this to check if an engine from another BMW model fits mine?

Yes — the checker will show you all engines that were originally fitted to your chassis. If an engine was offered in a different model but not yours, it won't appear. For cross-model swaps, consult a specialist and our engine code decoder.

What if my engine isn't listed?

If your engine code isn't shown, it may not have been factory-fitted to that chassis/year combination. Double-check your VIN and engine number using our engine number location guide, or contact one of our 20+ vetted specialists for confirmation.

Does this tool cover M models?

Yes — select M3, M4, M5, or other M models from the dropdown to see S-series engines like S55, S58, S63, etc.
